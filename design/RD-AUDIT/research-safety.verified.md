### SAF-V02 A node whose own row cannot fire in this world never completes
- What: Some nodes are unlocked only by a catalog row with `requires` (e.g. T-MAD ← F14 `e>0.6&l1>=0`, data findings.ts:44). Its ETA shows "next result", and nothing says the node itself cannot be reached.

- Severity: medium · Effort: S
