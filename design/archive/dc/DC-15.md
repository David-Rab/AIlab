# DC-15 A leased campus starts one financing lease per building, when that building goes live

## Decision (designer, 2026-11-27)
A campus paid with a financing lease (`pay: 'lease'`, the lessor funds `CAPEX_LEASE.siteShare` of capex) starts each
building's lease when **that building** goes live, not one lease at the last building (DC-13). A single building behaves
exactly as before.

## State
- `SiteWS3.lease` = `{ rate, funded, byBuilding[], booked }`. `byBuilding[k]` = what the lessor has funded for building k
  with construction interest; `funded` = their sum (kept for readers of the total: runway, UI); `booked` = buildings whose
  lease has started (they stop accruing interest).
- Funding: the lessor's share of the 5% commitment payment is split equally over the buildings; each construction payment
  (`buildStep`) funds the buildings it pays for (equal parts, as DC-13); the final top-up at the last building funds the
  buildings not yet booked. Interest compounds quarterly on each building's `byBuilding[k]` until its lease starts.
- Go-live of building k (`startLeases`, called from `buildStep` after the quarter's payments): one `finLease` ledger entry,
  `byBuilding[k] x CRF(rate, CAPEX_LEASE.years.site)` a year for 15 years from that day, one finance log line. Campus ids
  `siteLeaseId(site.id)-b{k}` with `contract = siteLeaseId(site.id)` (so the residual-value guarantee, the contracts table
  and deferrals group a campus's leases); a single building keeps `siteLeaseId(site.id)` and no `contract` (ledger
  unchanged).
- `goLive` no longer books a lease; the "span" approximation of `siteSchedule` and the last-building lease path are gone.

## Preview = effect
- `SiteSchedule` gains `etaQs?` (each building's go-live quarter; absent = `[etaQ]`); `etaQ` is the first building's,
  `buildQ` is one building's construction quarters (no span). `siteSchedule` returns `etaQs` for a campus.
- `constructionGrowth` = mean over buildings of `buildingGrowth(r, eta_k, sch)` (each building: 5%/n at commitment compounding
  to its go-live, 95%/n paid in equal parts over its own `buildQ` quarters). `payOptions` is unchanged (total funded x CRF
  = the sum of the booked leases). `pv.ts`: `siteCashFactor` is the mean over buildings, `siteLeasePV` splits the option's
  per-year amount over buildings in proportion to their growth and discounts each annuity from its own go-live quarter.
  One building reduces bit-for-bit to the old formulas.
- Residual-value guarantee (`contingentLiabilities`, `siteRVG`): ledger balances of started leases plus
  `unbookedFunded(site)` (what the lessor has funded for buildings with no lease yet), which also fixes a live campus still
  building (its later buildings were not counted before).
