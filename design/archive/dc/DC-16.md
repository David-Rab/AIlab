## Runway (item 5)
`finance/runway.ts`: a leased site projects one lease per building without a lease yet, each from its live day
(`ws3.liveDays`; permitting: `permits end + max(power, construction + k)` quarters), on `byBuilding[k]` plus an equal share
of what the lessor will still fund. A single building is unchanged (one lease from `end`). A campus with some buildings
live (stage `live`, last building not yet) is now projected (capex left and leases); capex for a campus is spread to its
last building, not its first. Checked: single building one flow; 5 x 1 GW five flows at +12..16 summing to one lease on
everything funded; after two buildings are live three remain; power-bound 3 x 300 MW three on one day.
