# DC-12 Size scaling: construction time, economies of scale, rushed gas in the backlog

## What changed
- `deals.ts`: `SCALE = { refGW: 1, buildTimeExp: 0.3, costExp: -0.1 }` ([design], pending research/23 §9); `LEVERS.crash` is now `{ buildMult: 0.5, buildingShare: 0.35, oddsMult: 1.5 }` (`buildQ: 4` dropped; sites.ts was its only reader).
- `sites.ts`: `siteBuildQ` = rent 0, else `max(1, round(8 x GW^0.3 x (crash ? 0.5 : 1)))`. `siteCapex`: the building part (grid/gas upfront) x `GW^-0.1`; crash and fast-track are shares of that scaled building; turbines, utility upgrades, rush, community and the rent network fit-out stay unscaled. `leverEffects`, the build preview and `newSite` all call these two functions (no copies).
- `waits.ts`: `inQueue` no longer excludes rushed gas; a rushed gas site is in the turbine backlog until its power is ready (replaces [D-5]).
- Glossary: `crashBuild`, `construction`, `rushPower`, `turbineBacklog`, `siteTier` rewritten (plain text, no live numbers).
