# DC-13 Campuses: buildings 1 / 3 / 5, phased go-live, repetition discount

## State
- `deals.ts`: `CAMPUS = { counts: [1, 3, 5], discount: { 1: 0, 3: 0.10, 5: 0.15 } }`, type `CampusBuildings`;
  `SITE_TIERS` = 100 / 300 / 1000 MW (the 2.5 and 5 GW tiles are gone); `SCALE = { refGW, buildTimeExp }` (`costExp`
  and its use removed).
- `SiteWS3` (all optional; absent = one building of `Site.mw`, i.e. the start sites): `buildings`, `buildingMW`,
  `liveDays` (planned go-live day of each building). `Site.mw` is the campus total.
- `SiteOpts.buildings?: 1 | 3 | 5` (default 1; rent forced 1). **`SiteOpts.mw` is the size of one building**; the site
  is `mw × buildings` (`campusMW`). Existing callers that pass only `mw` get one building of that size, as before.

## Per-building live days: stored [D]
`schedule()` (permits end) stores `ws3.liveDays[k] = quarter(permitsEnd + max(powerQ, buildQ + k))`, `liveAt =
liveDays[0]`; rent stores `[liveAt]`. Stored rather than derived so `siteLiveMW(site, day)` needs no game state and a
re-permit (E27, delays) simply rewrites them. Construction start of building k is derived: live day − buildQ quarters.

## Engine
- `siteLiveMW(site, day)` (power.ts, re-exported from sites.ts and the barrel) = buildingMW × buildings live by `day`;
  0 before the first; a live site without `liveDays` has all of `mw`. Stage becomes `live` with the first building.
- sitesQ keeps stepping a live campus until its last building is live (`stillBuilding`). `buildStep` replaces the
  building-stage payment: each building under construction pays an equal part of what it has left a quarter (capex
  left less a full 95%/n share per unstarted building, spread over the quarters they have left); one building reduces
  exactly to the old `(capex − paid) ÷ quarters left`. `goLive` (rest of capex, financing lease) runs at the last
  building.
- `siteCapex`: building × (1 − discount[n]); crash and fast-track shares of the discounted building; extra, rush,
  community per GW of the total, undiscounted. 5% of the total at commitment.
- New: `siteBuildings`, `campusMW`, `siteSizeError` (buildings not 1/3/5, or total over the source cap),
  `siteBuildingLiveInQ` (per building), `siteLastLiveInQ`; `siteLiveInQ` = first building. `LeverEffect.savesLastQ`
  added (savesQ stays first building). `newSite` clamps the building to [100 MW, cap] and drops to fewer buildings if
  the total is over the cap (the action refuses before that).
- `siteSchedule` (lease construction interest): etaQ = last building, buildQ = span from first construction start
  (payments approximated as equal over the span). **[D] a campus's financing lease starts at its last building**.

## MW readers changed (all use siteLiveMW)
installInventory (free racks), ownedOnline, powerAndOMPerYear (power on installed chips in live buildings; O&M on
capex × live MW ÷ total MW), chipFit, computeHistQ racks, computeForecast (racks per building quarter; one `siteLive`
event per site and quarter with the MW going live then, plus `buildings: { live, of }` for a campus), siteBars
(`lastLiveQ` for a phased campus; construction bar runs to it). New `siteBuildingLiveQs` (forecast; `siteLiveQ` = its
first). cmp.newSite preview: refused campus returns one cost line "cannot build: …" and no gains; apply returns the
reason; gains are one line per go-live quarter with that quarter's H100e; rent price and exposure on the total; capex
and lease lines lag to the last building. Unchanged on purpose (planned total): waits.ts queues, community action,
E27, opposition exposure.
